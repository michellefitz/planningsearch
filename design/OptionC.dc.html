Why

Matches the mental model — looking things up is a different job from managing what you keep. Scales cleanly: new personal features have an obvious home instead of lengthening a flat bar.

Tradeoff

Two levels of navigation for a product with six features may be premature. Pre-planning genuinely straddles the split — you run it while exploring, then want the report later.
