Why

Scannable: the eye finds the property without reading a paragraph. The card is a real object with a surface, and each one is a clean unit to save or open.

Tradeoff

Heavier — three of these in one answer is a wall of boxes. And the prose becomes dependent: “Refused because…” is stranded if the card fails to render or someone copies the text out.
