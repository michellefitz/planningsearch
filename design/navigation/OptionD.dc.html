Why

Collapses three features into one idea. "Everything about this site in one place" is a stronger promise than a list of tools, and it is how someone buying or building actually thinks.

Tradeoff

Biggest rebuild by far. Two things resist it: an area alert is not a property, and a pre-planning report for a site you have not saved needs a home. Both need answers before this is real.
