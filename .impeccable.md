# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
Irish planning-application trackers, in three registers: homeowners keeping an
eye on their own application or a neighbour's; construction and development
professionals tracking every live project of theirs in one place; researchers
and planning consultants assembling comparable applications for clients. The
account dashboard is a working surface they return to weekly or daily — not a
marketing page. Used at a desk during work hours, and on a phone when a
digest email lands.

### Brand Personality
Precise, professional, dense. A working tool for people who track planning
for a living. The interface should feel like it respects the user's time and
expertise: information-forward, numerate, unhurried but never slow. Emotion
to evoke: quiet command — "I know the state of everything I'm watching."

### Aesthetic Direction
Quiet utility chrome, already established in `web/src/styles.css`: hairline
borders carry structure, shadows are nearly invisible, motion is short and
eased-out (`--ease: cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`), numerals are tabular
wherever they are data. Brand navy `#17456e`, action blue `#0b62d6`, Inter
with disambiguated glyphs. Light theme (daytime professional use). Density is
a feature: prefer more information per screen over more whitespace, achieved
through hierarchy rather than clutter.

Anti-references — this must NOT feel like:
- A property listings site (Daft/MyHome commercial listing chrome)
- A data-nerd terminal (dense mono tables, dark mode, Bloomberg cosplay)
- A government portal (beige bureaucracy, PDF-adjacent, forms-first)

### Design Principles
1. Data earns the ink: every visual element carries information; decoration
   that conveys nothing is removed.
2. Hierarchy through type and hairlines, not boxes: avoid wrapping everything
   in cards; a rule and a weight change beat a container.
3. Status is the story: an application's canonical status (and what changed)
   is always the most legible thing on any surface.
4. Fast hands: frequent actions are instant and keyboard-reachable; motion is
   reserved for state changes and arrivals, never for chrome.
5. Empty states teach: a fresh account shows what the product does and how to
   start, in the product's own voice — encouraging, not cute.
